A half marathon is long enough that fuelling matters, but short enough that you don't need to overthink it.

## The night before

A normal, carb-heavy dinner you've eaten before. Nothing new.

## Race morning

Eat two to three hours before the start, then sip water.

## During the race

Most people do well with a gel or a few sweets around 45 to 60 minutes in. Practise it on your long runs first.
