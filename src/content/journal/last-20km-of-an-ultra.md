Somewhere past the marathon mark, an ultra stops being a running race and becomes a problem-solving one. What to eat, when to walk, how to keep moving when every part of you wants to sit down.

## Break it into pieces

Nobody runs 20 km at that point. You run to the next checkpoint, the next gate, the next tree.

## Keep eating

A common mistake is to stop fuelling because you don't feel like it. That's exactly when you need it.
