## Visiting this website

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

- **Fonts and images** are served from this website itself, so your browser doesn't contact Google or Instagram when you view a page.
- **The Instagram section** shows copies of my latest posts. Instagram only receives your information if you click through to Instagram, and then their own privacy policy applies.
- **The hosting provider** keeps short-lived technical logs (such as IP addresses and the pages requested) to keep the site secure and running. I don't use these logs to identify anyone.

## The enquiry form

If you use the enquiry form, your name, email, phone number (if given) and message are sent to me by email through Web3Forms, a form-handling service that processes it on my behalf. Their [privacy policy](https://web3forms.com/privacy) explains how they handle it. Please don't include health details in the form.

## When you get in touch or train with me

You contact me through the enquiry form, or by text, phone, email or Instagram message. I collect:

- **Contact details:** your name, phone number and email address.
- **Training information:** your goals, training history, availability, and progress such as times, distances and weights.
- **Health information:** a health questionnaire (PAR-Q), injuries, medical conditions and anything else that affects how you can safely train.
- **Payment records:** what you've paid and when. I don't store card details.

Health information is "special category" data. I only collect it with your explicit consent, and only so I can coach you safely. You can withdraw that consent at any time, but I may not be able to keep coaching you without it.

## Who I share it with

I only share your information when I need to:

- **Tools I use to run the business:** for example my phone, email provider, Web3Forms (for the enquiry form) <mark>[and any training app, e.g. TrainingPeaks, if used]</mark>. These providers store data on my behalf.
- **My accountant or HMRC** for tax purposes.
- **Emergency services** if you are injured or unwell during a session.

## How long I keep it

- Training and health information: until 12 months after you stop training with me, then deleted.
- Payment and accounting records: 6 years, as required by HMRC.
- Messages from people who don't go on to book: deleted within 12 months.

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- ask for a copy of the information I hold about you
- ask me to correct anything that's wrong
- ask me to delete your information (unless I have to keep it, for example for tax)
- object to, or ask me to limit, how I use your information
- withdraw consent at any time
- ask for your information in a format you can take elsewhere

To use any of these rights, email **Rothwell.pt@gmail.com**. I'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how I've handled your information, please tell me first so I can put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk) or on 0303 123 1113.
