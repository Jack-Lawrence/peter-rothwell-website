I want everyone who trains with me to feel it was worth it. This page explains when you can cancel and get your money back. It sits alongside the [Terms & Conditions](../terms/).

## Your 14-day right to cancel

Because you book by text, phone or email rather than face to face, the law gives you 14 days from the day your booking is confirmed to change your mind (Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013).

- **If we haven't started yet,** you'll get a full refund.
- **If you've asked to start within the 14 days,** you'll pay only for what you've used. For example, one PT session out of the month, or one week of online coaching. The rest is refunded.

To cancel, text **07367 636632** or email **Rothwell.pt@gmail.com** and say you want to cancel. You don't have to give a reason.

## After the first 14 days

### Monthly plans (online coaching and PT)

- You can stop at any time by telling me before your next payment date. You won't be charged again.
- The month you've already paid for isn't refundable, but you can use the rest of it.

### Run Club

- Cancel at least 7 days before the block starts for a full refund.
- After that, the block isn't refundable, but you can move your place to the next block <mark>[Peter to confirm]</mark>.

## When I'll always refund you

- If I cancel a session and we can't find another time, you'll get a refund for it.
- If I end your coaching early for any reason other than a breach of the terms, you'll get a refund for anything paid for but not received.
- If you have an injury or medical condition that stops you training, with a note from a doctor or physio, I'll pause your plan or refund the time you can't use <mark>[Peter to confirm]</mark>.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds go back by the same method you paid, within 14 days of us agreeing the refund.

## Not happy?

If something isn't right with your coaching, tell me. I'd much rather fix it. Your statutory rights, including services being provided with reasonable care and skill under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, aren't affected by this policy.
