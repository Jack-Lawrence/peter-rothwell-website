I want everyone to be able to use this website and find out about training with me, whatever device you use and however you browse.

## What I aim for

This website is built to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. In practice that means:

- **Keyboard:** everything works with a keyboard, there's a "Skip to content" link at the top of every page, and you can always see which item is selected.
- **Screen readers:** pages use proper headings, lists and landmarks, and images have text descriptions.
- **Contrast:** text has strong colour contrast against its background.
- **Zoom:** you can zoom to 200% or use your browser's text size settings without losing content. The layout works on phones, tablets and desktops.
- **Motion:** if your device is set to reduce motion, smooth scrolling and animations are switched off.
- **Speed:** there's no tracking, pop-ups or autoplaying video, so pages load quickly.

## Known issues

- Photos from Instagram use the post's caption as their description. Some captions may not fully describe the image.
- Some older blog posts may have images without full descriptions. I'm adding them as I go.

## Reporting a problem

If something on this website doesn't work for you, please let me know by email at **Rothwell.pt@gmail.com**. Tell me the page and what went wrong, and I'll aim to reply within 5 working days and fix it.
